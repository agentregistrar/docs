The Developer Portal at [agentregistrar.dev/dashboard](https://agentregistrar.dev/dashboard) is your control panel for every agent acting under your verified human account. You inspect agents, disconnect OAuth sessions, read live inboxes, audit every terms acceptance and signup in `/activity`, manage verification rules in `/trust`, and configure email forwarding in `/settings`.

## First-time onboarding

New developer accounts complete three one-time steps before connecting a coding tool:

1. **GitHub sign-in (`/login`):** sign in with GitHub. There is no separate username/password form. Your GitHub email becomes the verified human owner contact.
2. **Email verification (`/onboarding/verify-email`):** enter the six-digit code sent via Resend to your email address. Codes expire after ten minutes, and you can request a new code after a short cooldown.
3. **Master Agent Delegation Agreement (`/onboarding/tos`):** read and sign the Master Agreement (`v2026-10-01`). This one agreement authorizes your agents to accept standard service terms on your behalf. If a coding tool opens `/oauth/authorize` before you finish onboarding, you can verify your email and sign the agreement inline without losing your OAuth session.

## Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

The dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of your account:

- **Identity header:** confirms your verified human email and account state.
- **MCP setup banner:** copy-paste connection snippets for **Claude Code**, **OpenCode**, **Codex CLI**, **Cursor**, and **Gemini CLI**. Once you have connected at least one agent, the banner collapses automatically so your active agents stay at the top.
- **Active agents grid:** shows your current quota usage (for example `1 of 2 active`, or unlimited when no cap is set), each agent's status (`ACTIVE`, `REVOKED`, or `SUSPENDED`), ID, inbox address, scopes, a link to open `/agents/[id]`, and a one-click kill switch.
- **Recent activity preview:** displays the newest entries from `/activity` with a link to the full audit trail.

Agents are created when you approve a coding tool on the browser OAuth consent screen (`/oauth/authorize`), not via a standalone create button on the dashboard.

## Agent detail (`/agents/[id]`)

Opening any agent shows its identity header, session controls, kill switch, and three activity tabs:

- **Identity header:** agent name, status badge, copy buttons for the agent ID (`agt_...`) and inbox address (`<agent-slug>@agents.agentregistrar.dev`), harness label, and delegated scopes (`tos:accept`, `signup:free_tier`, `inbox:read`).
- **Rename and OAuth sessions:** rename the agent inline (updating its display label without changing its `agt_...` ID or email address), and review every connected OAuth session with a per-session **Disconnect** button. Disconnecting one session revokes that tool's bearer token without revoking the agent or affecting other connected tools.
- **Kill switch:** one-click **Revoke agent** / **Reactivate agent** button (hidden when an agent is under operator suspension).
- **Three tabs:**
  - **Live Inbox:** reads every inbound message delivered to the agent's address in real time, showing plain text, HTML, headers, and legal/terms flags.
  - **Activity:** filtered view of `/activity` showing only this agent's terms acceptances and signup receipts.
  - **Abuse Reports:** lists any authenticated abuse reports filed by providers against this agent, including standing (`verified` or `unverified`), reason, evidence JTI, and case details.

## Kill switch, suspension, and compromise response

### Revoke vs. operator suspension

| State | Who sets it | What stops immediately | How it is restored |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`revoked`** | Human owner via one-click **Revoke agent** in `/dashboard` or `/agents/[id]` | New `identity_signup` tokens, `identity_whoami`, and `inbox_read` for that agent. Other agents stay active. | Human clicks **Reactivate agent** (restores the exact same ID and inbox). |
| **`suspended`** | Broker operator following a verified, receipt-linked provider abuse report | Same tool blocks as `revoked`, plus new browser OAuth approvals on the account are blocked. | Operator review and restore only; the kill switch is hidden and cannot clear it. |

### What revocation does not do

The kill switch is a broker identity control, not a remote delete button on third-party services:

- It does **not** delete accounts the agent already created at providers.
- It does **not** revoke or rotate provider API keys (Agent Registrar never stored them).
- It does **not** erase recorded terms acceptances in `/activity`.
- It does **not** recall a signed identity token already sent to a provider (tokens expire within five minutes).

### If an agent or credential is compromised

1. Click **Revoke agent** in `/dashboard` or `/agents/[id]`, or **Disconnect** the affected OAuth session if only one tool's token leaked.
2. Open `/activity` to list every service the agent signed up for.
3. Rotate or revoke provider API keys directly in each affected provider's dashboard or API.
4. Inspect the agent's **Live Inbox** and **Activity** tabs for unexpected signups or verification emails.
5. Preserve the agent ID, assertion JTIs, and timestamps before deleting anything, and contact affected providers.
6. If your GitHub account or Agent Registrar account itself was compromised, secure GitHub and your email first, revoke all active agents, and report the incident to `privacy@agentregistrar.dev` (never send passwords, session cookies, or provider API keys).

## Activity (`/activity`)

`/activity` is your unified, paginated audit trail across all agents. Every row starts from a recorded terms acceptance (`tos_acceptances`) joined with its strongest signup receipt (if the provider issued one).

Because acceptances are the base of the table:

- **Keyless and receiptless signups stay visible.** Even when a provider verifies your agent's token without issuing a signed receipt, you still see the service, agent, terms URL, SHA-256 hash, timestamp, and email delivery status.
- **Receipt-backed signups show proof details.** When a provider posts a receipt (or your agent records one with `identity_record_signup`), the row includes the provider account ID, proof source (`rp_callback` or `rp_signed`), and optional hash-pinned guide link.
- **Verification snapshots stay transparent.** Each entry records the provider's verification status at signup time separately from its latest known status.

## Trust (`/trust` and `/trust/report`)

Every agent under your account inherits your trust policy from [agentregistrar.dev/trust](https://agentregistrar.dev/trust):

- **Default verification policy:**
  - **`verify` (recommended default):** requires a valid verified domain (or entity) credential from a trusted broker before minting a signup token. Accepts both broker-managed and provider-managed verification.
  - **`no_verify`:** allows signups to unverified services (the service is still labeled unverified in `/activity`).
  - **`strict`:** requires a valid broker-managed verification credential and rejects provider-managed sources.
- **Per-provider rules and blocklist:** override the default policy for a specific provider origin and audience (for example, allowing one local or unverified tool with `no_verify`), or mark any service as **blocked** (`rp_blocked` refuses signup regardless of verification status).
- **Submitted provider reports and `/trust/report`:** if a provider misbehaves, use [agentregistrar.dev/trust/report](https://agentregistrar.dev/trust/report) to submit a report naming the origin, audience, reason, and optional evidence. You can optionally block the provider or distrust its attesting broker in the same step. Provider reports open a case and update your personal policy; they do not adjudicate disputes.
- **Advanced: federated broker trust:** collapsed by default on `/trust`. Expand it to view broker defaults or explicitly trust or distrust another broker (for example, trusting the demo Broker B in [Try the demo network](/demo/)).

<Aside type="note" title="Verification is identity, not safety">
A verified provider proved control of its domain (or legal-entity details at the `entity` level). It is not an endorsement, security audit, or safety guarantee.
</Aside>

## Settings (`/settings`)

[agentregistrar.dev/settings](https://agentregistrar.dev/settings) manages two areas:

- **Email forwarding mode:** choose how inbound emails sent to your agents' `@agents.agentregistrar.dev` addresses are forwarded to your verified email: **`all`** (default), **`legal_only`**, or **`off`**. Terms acceptance copies are **always** emailed to you regardless of this setting. See [Inbox and verification](/agents/inbox-and-verification/) for full details.
- **Account and Delegation Agreement (read-only):** confirms your verified email address, active agent quota (`X of 2` or unlimited), signed Master Delegation Agreement version (`v2026-10-01`), signing timestamp, and agreement SHA-256 hash.
