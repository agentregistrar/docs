Brokered identity for AI agents: every agent acts under a verified human owner.

Agent Registrar is an agent identity and brokerage service. A verified human signs one master delegation agreement; each agent gets a sub-identity, a dedicated email inbox, and scoped delegation. A relying party publishes an identity-only discovery document and verifies a signed assertion; the broker records the ToS acceptance and the signup receipt.

## How it works

1. The human verifies an email address and signs the master delegation agreement.
2. The agent registers a sub-identity with a dedicated inbox address.
3. The agent reads the provider discovery document and recomputes the terms hash.
4. `identity_prove` records the acceptance and mints a short-lived signed assertion.
5. The provider verifies the assertion, creates the account and returns a receipt.

[How the handshake works](/start/how-it-works/) has the diagram, the numbered flow and the claim-by-claim detail.

<Aside type="caution" title="Known limits">
A malicious relying party can harvest contact details, and discovery conformance is cheap to publish. Revocation suspends the broker identity but does not delete accounts a provider already created. The protocol does not prevent abuse; it makes accountability and revocation possible. [Trust and limits](/start/trust-and-limits/) states every known gap.
</Aside>
