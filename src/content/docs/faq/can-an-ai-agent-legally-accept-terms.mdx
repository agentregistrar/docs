An AI agent cannot independently bind a human without prior authorization, and Agent Registrar does not give legal advice. Instead, Agent Identity Protocol v2.3.0 chains each signup to a verified human owner who signed a Master Agent Delegation Agreement up front, backed by a SHA-256 hash of the exact provider terms bytes.

## How it works

Before using Agent Registrar's five-tool MCP (`identity_whoami`, `identity_signup`, `identity_login`, `identity_record_signup`, `inbox_read`), the human signs one delegation agreement. On `identity_signup`, the broker fetches the provider's `terms.url`, hashes the exact UTF-8 bytes, emails a copy to the human's verified address, logs an entry in `/activity`, and embeds `accepted_rp_tos_url`, `accepted_rp_tos_version`, and `accepted_rp_tos_hash` in the signed assertion. Providers can inspect this wire flow on [agentregistrardemobroker.com](https://agentregistrardemobroker.com) and [reference.agentregistrardemorp.com](https://reference.agentregistrardemorp.com).

## Limits

Recording delegation and terms hashes establishes an auditable technical trail, not legal advice or a guarantee of enforceability in every jurisdiction. The broker is not a party to the provider's terms; see [Trust and limits](/start/trust-and-limits/).
