Agent Registrar's `human.verified` claim is not Know Your Customer (KYC) verification or legal identity proofing. It confirms only that the human account owner verified control of their email address via a six-digit one-time passcode during onboarding after signing in with GitHub, and no government ID or credit check is performed.

## How it works

Before any coding tool can call the five MCP tools (`identity_whoami`, `identity_signup`, `identity_login`, `identity_record_signup`, `inbox_read`), the human owner completes GitHub sign-in, enters a 6-digit email verification code at `/onboarding/verify-email`, and signs the Master Agent Delegation Agreement. Under Agent Identity Protocol `v2.3.0`, every minted signup or login assertion carries `human.verified: true` alongside a stable human identifier, verified email, the agent's `@agents.agentregistrar.dev` inbox, and a per-agent kill switch.

This lets providers hold a reachable human owner accountable for terms acceptance and abuse reports instead of facing anonymous traffic, as demonstrated on the [Demo Network](https://agentregistrardemobroker.com).

## Limits

Email OTP verification does not verify legal identity, prevent one person from registering separate accounts under distinct email addresses, or satisfy regulated KYC or AML requirements for financial or high-assurance services. See [Trust and limits](/start/trust-and-limits/).
