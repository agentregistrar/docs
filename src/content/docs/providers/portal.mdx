The Provider Portal at [agentregistrar.dev/provider/new](https://agentregistrar.dev/provider/new) guides you through verifying your domain, generating your discovery document, testing your signup endpoint, viewing recorded signups, and managing optional signing keys and teammates.

You do not need a provider account to verify assertions against public JWKS, but completing the portal setup makes your service a verified provider for clients running the default `verify` policy and `strict` policy. Every onboarding step works on the free plan.

## Create a provider organization

1. Sign in with your verified human account and open [agentregistrar.dev/provider/new](https://agentregistrar.dev/provider/new).
2. Enter your organization display name, permanent URL slug, and contact email.
3. Verify your organization contact email via the link sent to that address (you can also re-send or update it later under **Settings**). An unverified contact email does not block setup, but it blocks marking a service live.

Once created, your workspace lives at `/provider/[slug]` with five tabs: **Services**, **Signups**, **Credentials**, **Team**, and **Settings**.

## Services tab

Route: `/provider/[slug]` and `/provider/[slug]/services/[id]`

The **Services** tab lists every service in your organization and walks each new service through a linear 3-step setup wizard.

### Add a service

Click **Add service** and enter three fields:

- **Display name:** Human-readable product name shown to users.
- **Origin:** Your canonical HTTPS origin (no path, query, fragment, or wildcard).
- **Audience:** A stable lowercase protocol identifier (3 to 63 characters: letters, numbers, and hyphens). The form suggests one from your origin domain.

Pending claims expire after 7 days and do not reserve an audience until you verify your domain. Once an agent mints a first assertion for a service, its audience cannot be changed.

### 3-step setup wizard

Opening a service at `/provider/[slug]/services/[id]` displays three steps in order:

1. **Verify domain:** Choose **HTTPS file** or **DNS TXT**, click **Create challenge**, publish the one-time proof token, and click **Verify**.
   - **HTTPS file:** Serve the exact token (optionally with one trailing newline) as `text/plain` with HTTP `200` at the shown well-known URL, with no cross-origin redirects.
   - **DNS TXT:** Publish exactly one `agent-signup-verify` TXT value at the shown record name.
   - Challenge tokens are shown once at creation and allow up to 5 verification attempts before expiry. Verifying your first origin activates the provider organization.
2. **Publish discovery:** Customize `terms.version`, `terms.url`, and `endpoints.agent_signup` in the browser-side generator, then copy the generated JSON and serve it at `GET /.well-known/agent-signup.json` with `Content-Type: application/json`.
   - The generated document includes your **broker-managed** `attestation` URL (`mode: "broker"`). No signing key is required (`keys: []` is supported by default), and the broker renews the attestation on demand.
3. **Test setup:** Click **Test my setup** to run a synchronous dry-run. The broker fetches your live `/.well-known/agent-signup.json`, checks the attestation and `terms.url`, probes `endpoints.agent_signup` with an unsigned request (requiring a stable JSON error envelope rather than HTML, a redirect, or `200`), and displays the exact placeholder assertion claims it would mint.
   - The dry-run creates no account and mints no assertion. When all checks pass and your organization contact email is verified, the service is marked live automatically.

### Operations and optional settings

Below the wizard, each service page exposes operational controls:

- **Health checks:** Daily scheduled checks start once a service is live. Owners and admins can also queue a manual **Check now** run (up to 24 manual runs per service in 24 hours). Health checks are informational and notify-only: they never alter trust status, never remove live status, and are not an uptime guarantee.
- **Optional signing keys:** Links to the **Credentials** tab if you want to add an Ed25519 key for signed receipts or Contract D.2 abuse reporting.
- **Edit metadata:** Update display name or non-frozen metadata.
- **Discard draft or retire service:** Discarding a pending draft or retiring a live service frees its plan slot and retires its origins in the same transaction. Confirm by typing the service `audience`. Existing keys and historical attestations stay intact so past receipts remain verifiable, and any audience that already minted an assertion remains claimed.

## Signups tab

Route: `/provider/[slug]/signups`

The **Signups** tab displays one row per logical signup recorded for your organization, scoped strictly to your own services. Unconverted assertion handoffs and raw JWTs are never shown.

Each row includes the service and origin, agent ID and inbox address, shared human name and email, accepted terms (`version`, `url`, `sha256`), your opaque `account_id`, signup-time verification status alongside current attestation status, and any account conflict flag.

Proof strength is labeled explicitly on every row:

| Proof label | Wire `proof_source` | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Provider-reported** | `rp_callback` | Strongest: your server delivered an RP-signed receipt directly via Contract F. |
| **Provider-signed** | `rp_signed` | Verified: the agent recorded an RP-signed receipt via `identity_record_signup`. |
| **Self-report** | `agent_self_reported` | Weaker historical or unverified claim with no provider signature. |

If a stronger receipt arrives for the same agent, audience, and account, the ledger displays the strongest proof and folds weaker entries into an expandable history row without double-counting the signup.

Click **Export CSV** to download `<slug>-signups.csv` directly in your browser. The export uses RFC 4180 quoting over the rendered rows and makes no additional network request.

## Credentials tab

Route: `/provider/[slug]/credentials`

The **Credentials** tab manages two separate credential types. Only owners and admins can create, retire, or revoke credentials.

### Optional RP signing keys

You do not need an RP signing key to accept agent signups. Generate and register an Ed25519 key pair here only if you want to issue RP-signed signup receipts (Contract E and F) or submit authenticated abuse reports (Contract D.2).

- Private key generation happens in your browser; the broker stores only your public key and `kid`.
- Keys support `active`, `retired`, and `revoked` states. Rotating keys with overlap keeps historical receipts verifiable while removing retired keys from new attestations.
- Even if a provider account is suspended, owners and admins can still revoke a compromised key.

### CI and automation API keys

Create scoped bearer API keys for CI pipelines or backend automation calling the provider HTTP API (such as renewing a provider-managed attestation via `POST /api/provider/v1/attestation`).

| Scope | Permission |
| :--- | :--- |
| `account:read` | Read provider account, status, plan, and entitlements. |
| `keys:write` | Request key challenges and register, retire, or revoke RP keys. |
| `attestation:read` | Read the current provider attestation. |
| `attestation:write` | Issue or renew a provider attestation. |

The full API key secret is shown **once** at creation and cannot be recovered. Store it in your secret manager and revoke unused keys immediately.

## Team tab

Route: `/provider/[slug]/members`

Manage organization membership across three roles: `owner`, `admin`, and `member`.

- **Email invitations:** Membership starts only when the invited person accepts an emailed link. Owners can invite `admin` or `member`; admins can invite `member`. Invitations expire after 7 days (to resend, revoke the pending invite and create a new one).
- **Roles and offboarding:** Owners can change member roles and remove admins or members; admins can remove members; non-owners can leave at any time. Removing a member ends portal access immediately but does not automatically revoke API keys: review and rotate any keys that person created under **Credentials**.
- **Ownership transfer:** Two-party transfer initiated by the current owner (confirmed by typing the organization slug). The target member accepts or declines on the **Team** page; accepting promotes them to sole `owner` and demotes the previous owner to `admin`.

## Settings tab

Route: `/provider/[slug]/settings`

- **Organization details:** Owners and admins can rename the organization display name. The organization slug is permanent.
- **Contact email:** Only the owner can change the organization contact email or re-send verification links (24-hour single-use link, 60-second resend cooldown). Changing the address clears verification until the new address is confirmed.
- **Plan and limits:** Displays your operator-assigned plan (`free` or `pro`), enabled feature flags, and current usage against limits (live services, member seats where the owner does not consume a seat, live origins per service, and check history retention window). There is no self-serve checkout, pricing page, or invoice flow in this release; contact the broker operator if you need higher service or origin limits.
