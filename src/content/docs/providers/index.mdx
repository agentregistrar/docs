Let AI agents sign up directly on your own domain without handing your customer relationship to a marketplace. Every agent arrives with a signed identity token backed by a verified human owner and a hash-pinned record of the exact terms accepted at signup.

Agent Registrar is not a marketplace, directory, or proxy. You keep your domain, signup API, account model, credentials, pricing, and user experience. The [Agent Identity Protocol](/protocol/) covers identity and terms acceptance; your service owns everything after signup.

## Choose your setup path

Two adoption paths are supported. Pick the one that matches how you want clients to trust your service.

| Path | Setup | How clients treat your service |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Provider Portal (recommended)** | Create a free provider organization at [agentregistrar.dev/provider/new](https://agentregistrar.dev/provider/new), add your service, and complete the 3-step setup wizard (verify domain, publish discovery, test setup). | Recognized as a **verified domain** by clients running the default `verify` policy and `strict` policy. Gives you the signup dashboard, optional signing keys, and authenticated abuse reporting. |
| **Permissionless DIY** | Publish `GET /.well-known/agent-signup.json` and verify incoming signed identity tokens against `https://agentregistrar.dev/.well-known/jwks.json`. No broker account and no SDK required. | Conformant and **unverified**. Works for agents running under `no_verify`; refused by clients running the default `verify` policy or `strict` policy. |

## What you publish and keep

- **One discovery file:** Serve `GET /.well-known/agent-signup.json` listing your service identifier, accepted broker, terms URL, and signup endpoint.
- **One signup endpoint:** Verify the incoming signed identity token against the broker's public keys, check the accepted terms URL, and create or resolve your own account.
- **Full product ownership:** Credentials, API keys, billing, rate limits, and post-signup documentation stay on your origin. Agent Registrar never stores or proxies your customer credentials.
- **No signing key needed to start:** Accepting agent signups only requires reading the broker's public keys. Add a provider signing key later only if you want signed signup receipts or authenticated abuse reports.

<Aside type="note" title="What domain verification covers">
Domain verification proves control of the domain at verification time. It is an identity check, not a safety certificate, uptime guarantee, or product endorsement. See [Trust and limits](/start/trust-and-limits/) for the full accountability model and [Demo network](/demo/) to inspect live reference services.
