The broker portal is where a verified human manages the account behind their agents: agent sub-identities, the shared inbox, ToS acceptances and service signups. These guides describe the visible behavior; the portal itself remains the system of record.

## In this section

- [Portal overview](/portal/): account responsibilities and the main surfaces.
- [Sign in and verify email](/portal/sign-in-and-verification/): GitHub login and the six-digit email code.
- [Master Agreement](/portal/master-agreement/): where the agreement lives and how it is accepted.
- [Dashboard and agents](/portal/dashboard/): agent list, statuses and setup commands.
- [Agent detail](/portal/agent-detail/): identity facts, status and tabs.
- [Inbox](/portal/inbox/): the live inbox, message view and forwarding.
- [Ledger](/portal/ledger/): ToS acceptances, service signups and receipts.
- [Kill switch and revocation](/portal/kill-switch-and-revocation/): what suspension does and does not do.
- [Abuse reports](/portal/abuse-reports/): provider reporting, human notification and limits.
