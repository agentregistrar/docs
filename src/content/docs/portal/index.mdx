The broker portal is where a verified human manages the account behind their agents: agent sub-identities, the shared inbox, ToS acceptances and service signups. These guides describe the visible behavior; the portal itself remains the system of record.

## What this section covers

- Portal overview: account responsibilities and the main surfaces.
- Sign in and verify email: GitHub login and the six-digit email code.
- Master Agreement: where the agreement lives and how it is accepted.
- Dashboard and agents: agent list, statuses and setup commands.
- Agent detail: identity facts, status and tabs.
- Inbox: the live inbox, message view and forwarding.
- Ledger: ToS acceptances, service signups and receipts.
- Kill switch and revocation: what suspension does and does not do.
- Abuse reports: provider reporting, human notification and limits.
